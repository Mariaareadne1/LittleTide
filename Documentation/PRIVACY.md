# Little Tide privacy policy

*Effective 16 November 2026*

Little Tide is a tide-prediction app. It has **no accounts, advertising, analytics, crash
reporting or tracking**, and it contains no third-party SDKs.

## What leaves your device
To show predictions, Little Tide requests data from the public NOAA Tides & Currents API
(`api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov`). A request contains:
- the NOAA station ID you chose,
- a date range, units and datum,
- an application name, `LittleTide_iOS`.

As with any internet request, NOAA's servers receive your device's IP address. Little Tide
sends no name, email, location, device identifier or other personal information. NOAA's
handling of requests is described in its own privacy policy.

## What stays on your device
- the coast you chose, your units and display preferences;
- saved NOAA predictions, so the app and its widgets work offline.

These are stored in a container shared only between Little Tide and its own widgets.
Nothing is uploaded to us, because we run no servers.

## What Little Tide does not use
Location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data, or any identifier for advertising.

## Your choices
- **Settings → Clear saved predictions** removes cached predictions.
- Deleting the app removes all of its data.

## Children
Little Tide collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
