## What competitors do well
- **Tide Guide** is a polished, dense instrument: full-screen charts, 10-day and 12-month
  tables, sun/moon, Live Activities, Watch/iPad/Mac. Its design earned Editors' Choice.
- **Ocean Watch** is already free, NOAA-based, collects no data, works offline, and covers
  Lock Screen, StandBy and Watch. Little Tide must not claim "free" or "private" as unique.
- **TidesPro** and NOAA show observed water levels; Little Tide deliberately does not.

## Where Little Tide is different
- A painted aerial coast whose illustrative waterline follows the predicted level. None of
  the listings above describe anything comparable.
- One calm coast at a time, US NOAA predictions only, with honest labels.
- Widgets are free (Tide Guide and TidesPro gate theirs behind a subscription).

## Small gaps a stranger would expect (V1-sized)
1. Switching quickly between a few coasts (every competitor has favorites).
2. A Lock Screen widget for the next tide.
3. Sunrise/sunset marks on the curve (deferred: scope).
4. Multi-day browsing (Little Tide pages −1…+6 days; no list view).
