## Common questions
**Why can't I find my beach?** Little Tide uses NOAA tide-prediction stations, which are
named for harbors, piers and inlets. Search for a nearby town, bay or your state, or browse
by state.

**Why is there no curve for my station?** It is a *subordinate* station. NOAA publishes only
its high and low times and heights, so Little Tide shows exactly those and nothing in between.

**The times look different from my phone's time zone.** Little Tide always shows the
station's own local time, so the times are right for anyone standing there.

**The widget says to open the app.** Its saved predictions have run out. Open Little Tide
with an internet connection to download new ones. iOS decides exactly when widgets refresh.

**Is this accurate enough for boating?** No. These are astronomical predictions, and weather
can change actual water levels. Little Tide is not for navigation or safety decisions.

**How do I add a widget?** Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap **Edit → Add Widget**,
search for *Little Tide*, choose small or medium, then tap **Add Widget**.
