# Design system

Three coordinated layers: the painted coast, the glass that sits on it, and the type and
information on the glass.

## Layer A: coastal artwork
- **Source:** original procedural paintings generated by ArtForge (no photographs, no AI
  imagery; see `IMAGE_CREDITS.md`). Three scenes: `eastCoast` (sandy point, dunes,
  sandbars), `norCal` (rocky cove, kelp, cold water), `soCal` (warm sand below a bluff,
  turquoise reefs). Regions map to scenes by `CoastRegion`; it is representative, never a
  map of the station.
- **Layers:** land (sand, grass, rocks, people above the high-tide line), water (depth
  colour, swell, glitter, craft in deep water), data (height field, foam masks). One
  shader composites them for any predicted level, so the same terrain shows low and high
  water; no image is stretched to fake the tide.
- **Motion:** slow swell drift, foam lace and caustics at 30 fps; paused under Reduce
  Motion, when inactive, and when scrolled away. The art stands still and complete.
- **Honesty:** the waterline is labelled "Illustrative waterline". It follows the
  predicted level within the day's range; it never claims a real shoreline position.

## Layer B: liquid glass
- Rendered inside the scene shader so it bends the actual painting (glass-studies port):
  squircle-bevel SDF, refraction at IOR 1.52, chromatic split along the displacement,
  golden-angle frost, Fresnel reflection, deep-teal absorption, milky rim, glints and a
  contact shadow. Only refraction moves pixels; light terms add exposure.
- Two materials: **card** (frost 0.62, bevel 26) for information, **button** (frost 0.3,
  stronger specular) for controls. Used only for the tide card, header controls, day pager,
  details panels, onboarding card and the caption pill.
- Reduce Transparency: opaque `oceanDeep` panels with a white hairline.

## Layer C: typography and information
- **Newsreader** (variable serif, SIL OFL 1.1) for place names, headline tide times, section
  headings and event titles; **SF Pro** (system) for labels, data and controls. Canela was
  considered and not used: no verified app-embedding licence (`TYPOGRAPHY.md`).
- Styles (`TideUI/Typography.swift`): `brand`, `heroLocation`, `heroTideTime`,
  `sectionHeading`, `eventTitle`, `body`, `bodyEmphasis`, `data` (tabular), `label`,
  `regionLabel` (tracked caps), `metadata`, `chartLabel`, widget styles. Serif styles scale
  through `UIFontMetrics` with matching optical size.
- Hierarchy on the hero: region label → place (serif) → card: direction and source →
  "High at 1:04 PM" (serif) → countdown, height, zone → freshness when relevant.
- Large text: rows and the readout reflow with `ViewThatFits`; the hero grows rather than
  truncate; chart labels cap at xLarge (the readout repeats them at full size).

## Colour tokens (`TideUI/Palette.swift`)
| Token | Use |
|---|---|
| `oceanDeep` #0C3E4C, `oceanMid` #135A68 | scrims, glass tint, page ground under the art |
| `onArt` white, `onArtSoft` white 82 % | all type on art and glass |
| `coral` #FAB087 | selected time, scrub marker, non-now day label |
| `paper`, `ink`, `inkSoft`, `seaGlass`, `sand` (light/dark) | sheets: search, settings, source |

Contrast is measured on rendered pixels in the UI audits (≥ 4.5:1; lowest measured 4.53:1
before the onboarding halo, now higher).

## App icon
A figure-free crop of the painted East Coast at mid tide: sand, the foam band and open water
with two rocks, so the icon, hero and widgets share one picture. Dark deepens sand and water
but keeps the foam bright; tinted is the painting's luminance. Generated by
`art-forge --icon` (no alpha channel, 1024 px).

## Widgets
- Small/medium: painted snapshot at the entry's predicted level, teal scrim where type sits,
  serif hero time, rhythm strip on medium. Tinted/clear: line art and system colours with
  the key text `widgetAccentable`.
- Lock Screen: inline, circular gauge (level within the next 24 h, only when NOAA publishes
  a height), rectangular (place, next event, direction and height). System monochrome.
