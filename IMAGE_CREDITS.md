Little Tide V1 ships **no photographs and no third-party images.**

| Asset | Where | Origin | Licence |

The illustrations are labelled in the app as *illustrations representative of a kind of
coast*. They are never presented as photographs or live views of the station.
No AI-generated imagery is used.

## If photographs are added later
Record for each one: file, creator, source URL, exact licence and version, date
permission was confirmed, required attribution text, and which station(s) or region it
genuinely depicts. Prefer original photographs or public-domain US government works
(e.g. NOAA, NPS). Check each item's terms, because not every image on a .gov site is
public domain. Show "Representative photo" unless it depicts the station itself.
